Ingredients
    For the Pull-Apart Bread
  • 16.3 oz can Grands biscuit dough (8 pieces)
  • U¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Cream cheese (softened at room temperature for at least 30 mins)
  • Milk
  • Vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar
    For the Pizza Dip
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce or pizza sauce
  • 1½ cups grated mozzarella cheese
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered (optional)
  • In a bowl, combine the melted butter, Parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning. Roll each biscuit dough ball in the seasoned butter, then arrange them around the edge of a greased large cast-iron skillet.
  • In a large bowl, use an electric mixer or stand mixer to combine the cream cheese, ricotta cheese, and 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning.
  • Spread the cream cheese mixture in the center of the skillet, inside the circle of biscuit dough. Top with marinara sauce, shredded mozzarella cheese, and the remaining 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning. Add pepperoni or another pizza topping of your choice.
  • Oven Temperature: 350°F (175°C)
  • Cooking Time: 25–30 minutes
  • Bake until: The bread is golden brown and the cheese is melted.
